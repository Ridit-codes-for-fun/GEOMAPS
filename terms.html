2. What the app does

The app is a free tool for planning walks in Mumbai. It shows walking routes on an OpenStreetMap-based map, with model forecasts of weather, air quality and heat. It doesn't provide driving, cycling or public-transport directions or live traffic. Mumbai Copilot is a rule-based helper: it doesn't use an AI model. It answers only by running the app's own tools (search, routing, weather, air quality, heat, facilities) and can misunderstand a question, so check its answers on the map.

4. Important limitations: please read

  • Routes are calculated from OpenStreetMap data that may be incomplete or out of date. A mapped path may be closed, private, under construction, flooded, poorly lit, unsafe or not suitable for you. Always use your own judgement and follow signs, barriers and local conditions.
  • Walking times and arrival times are estimates based on an average pace of 4.8 km/h, with no allowance for waiting, crowds or your own speed.
  • Weather, air-quality and heat information comes from numerical forecast models (Open-Meteo and CAMS). It describes areas several kilometres across, not your exact street, and forecasts can be wrong.
  • Heat categories are this app's own estimates. They are not official heatwave warnings, which only the India Meteorological Department (IMD) issues. Air-quality values use the US EPA scale, not India's National AQI.
  • Parking, EV charging and toilets come from OpenStreetMap via the Overpass API. They show where facilities are mapped, not whether they are open, public, free or have space. Availability is always unknown.
  • Public places shown on the map (parks, hospitals, libraries, malls) come from OpenStreetMap. Their opening hours, accessibility and whether they offer cooling are not verified.
  • Simulated walks, including following a route in the 3D view, are previews, not your real position. The 3D view is drawn from OpenStreetMap data, not real street imagery. Building heights are shown only where mapped, or estimated from the number of floors (3 m per floor). Buildings without either are drawn flat.

We don't guarantee that any route is safe, that any forecast is accurate, or that the app will be available, uninterrupted or error-free.
