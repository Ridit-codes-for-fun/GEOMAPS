Cookie & Storage Policy

Policy version 1.2 · Last updated 23 October 2026

Summary

1. This site sets no cookies

HeatShield Mumbai doesn't create cookies, and has no analytics, advertising, social-media plug-ins or other tracking technologies. Requests the app makes to Open-Meteo, the Overpass API and OpenStreetMap Nominatim are sent without cookies, so those services can't set or read cookies through this app.

2. Cookies, local storage, session storage and IndexedDB

  • Cookies are small files a website sets that the browser sends back to the server with every request. They are often used for logins and tracking. This app uses none.
  • Local storage keeps data in your browser for this site only. It is never sent to a server automatically and stays until deleted. This app uses it, with your permission, as described below.
  • Session storage is like local storage but is cleared when the tab closes. This app doesn't use it.
  • IndexedDB is a larger in-browser database. This app doesn't use it.
  • Your browser's normal HTTP cache also keeps copies of the app's own files. For example, the map data file is kept for up to one day so it doesn't download every time. Your browser manages this. It contains no personal data and can be cleared in your browser settings.

4. Your choices

On your first visit a notice offers Accept all, Reject optional and Customise. The optional switches start off. To change or withdraw your choice at any time, use Storage settings in the footer of every page or in the app's Settings panel. Switching a category off deletes its data immediately. "Delete everything this app stored on this device" removes every entry above, including your choice.

You can also clear site data in your browser settings or use a private window. Doing either removes everything listed here.

5. Changes

If the app starts storing something new, this page and its version number will change, and the app will ask for your choice again before storing it.
