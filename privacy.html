2. The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Our server only delivers the app's files. It doesn't receive your location, searches or routes. Route calculation, place search and map drawing run inside your browser.
  • Outside services are contacted directly from your browser: Open-Meteo, for weather, air quality and heat; the Overpass API, only when you turn on parking, EV charging or toilets, ask the Copilot for them, or open the 3D view; and OpenStreetMap Nominatim, only when you choose an online search.
  • Settings and cached results are kept in your browser only if you allow it, and you can delete them at any time.

5. Legal basis and consent

Sending coordinates to get weather, and search text to find a place, happens only because you asked the app to do that. Storing settings and cached results on your device happens only with the consent you give in the storage notice. You can withdraw that consent as easily as you gave it: open Storage settings, from the footer or the app's Settings panel, and switch the option off. Withdrawing deletes the stored data immediately. It doesn't affect anything done before you withdrew.

6. How long data is kept

  • Location, routes and searches in the page's memory: until you close or reload the page.
  • Cached results (including facility map areas): deleted the next time the app opens after 7 days, or immediately when you withdraw cache consent. Copilot chat: until you close or reload the page.
  • Settings: until you change them, withdraw consent or clear your browser data.
  • Your storage choice: 12 months. After that, or when this policy's version changes, the app asks again and removes optional data until you decide.
  • Data held by Open-Meteo, Nominatim or the host: according to their policies.

7. Security

All requests to outside services use HTTPS. The site sends security headers, including a Content Security Policy that only allows connections to the services named here. Data in local storage is not encrypted. Anyone with access to your browser profile can read it, so use "Delete everything this app stored on this device" on shared computers. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

8. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), you can ask for a summary of your personal data being processed, its correction, completion, updating or erasure, and the redress of grievances. You can also nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapable. Because we don't hold your data on our servers, you can do most of this yourself:

  • See what is stored: Storage settings shows a count. Your browser's developer tools show the entries (keys starting with mnav).
  • Erase: "Delete everything this app stored on this device" in Storage settings.
  • Withdraw consent: switch the option off in Storage settings.

10. The law and when it applies

This policy is written with the DPDP Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 in mind. The Rules were notified on 13 November 2025 and come into force in phases. Most obligations on data fiduciaries, including notice, consent, security and data-principal rights, apply from about 13 May 2027. We follow them already. Other laws, such as the Information Technology Act, 2000, may also apply. This policy was generated as part of building the app and is not a guarantee of legal compliance. It must be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before public launch.
