Teaching model of a made-up neighbourhood. Click a place, plan a cooler route, or edit the map.

This is not Mumbai and not real data. The neighbourhood is fictional and every number comes from the sliders and a simple teaching model, to show how shade, surfaces and green space change heat, flooding and air pollution. For real Mumbai routes, live weather and the heat forecast map, use Mumbai navigation.
